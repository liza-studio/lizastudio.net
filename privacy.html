Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Liza Studio ("we") handles, why, and what choices you have. It covers the website lizastudio.net and the Liza service.

1. This website

The website has no analytics, no advertising trackers and sets no cookies. Like any website, the hosting provider records technical request logs (IP address, time, page requested) for security and reliability.

If you write to us by email, we receive your address and the content of your message and use them only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation.

2. Liza for individuals

When you use Liza in a messenger, we process the messages and files you send, the identifier and display name the messenger provides, and the memory Liza builds from your conversations. We use this data to answer your requests and to keep context between conversations.

To produce answers, message content is sent to the AI model providers that power the agents. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

3. Liza for Business

For business customers Liza runs on a server dedicated to that customer. Conversations, files and memory are stored on that server. The customer decides who has access and how long data is kept; we act on the customer's instructions under the service agreement.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep data for as long as you use the service. You can ask us to export or delete your data at any time by writing to the address below; we act on such requests within 30 days.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above.
